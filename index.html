My PADI Diver license taught me the most important rules: "Buddy Check" and "Strictly follow SOP."

Underwater, breaking rules can cost lives; at work, breaking SOPs can cause accidents.

Q7: How do you resolve technical conflicts?

My core rule is: "Focus on the issue, not the person, and let data and SOP speak."

I will listen to their ideas first, then open the engineering drawings or manuals to check together objectively.

Q8: How do you handle stress?

I handle stress through "physical release" (working out to reset) and "solving the root cause."

Stress comes from uncertainty. I calm down, read the Manual, or discuss with seniors. Once solved, stress goes away.
